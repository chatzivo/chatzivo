Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about getting started.

Is Chatzivo free?

This starter demo has no payment flow.

Do I need my real name?

No. Choose a nickname and protect your personal information.

Can other people see my messages?

Yes. Messages are public to visitors who join the same room. Do not share private information.

Can I use Chatzivo worldwide?

Visitors can join from different places. Please keep conversations respectful and follow the community guidelines.
